# Privacy Policy — FreshCart

**Last updated: November 6, 2026**

FreshCart ("the App") is a simple grocery list application.

## Information we collect

**We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.**

- Your grocery list is stored **only on your device**, in the App's local storage.
- The App does **not** use the internet — it works completely offline.
- The App requests **no** Android permissions (no camera, contacts, location, microphone, etc.).
- The App contains **no** advertising, **no** analytics, **no** tracking, **no** accounts, and **no** in-app purchases.

## Data deletion

Because all data lives only on your device:

- Deleting an item, using "Delete got items", or "Delete entire list" removes it permanently from the App.
- Uninstalling the App removes all of its local data.
- We never receive your data, so we cannot access, correct, or delete it for you.

## Children's privacy

The App does not collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

Any updates to this policy will be posted on the page where this policy is published. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance.
